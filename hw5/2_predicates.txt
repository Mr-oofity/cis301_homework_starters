Quantifiers - helps describe all or some elements in a set

    ∀ - universal quantifier, "for all", "all"
        describing something about ALL elements in a set

    ∃ - existential quantifier, "there exists",
        "some", "at least one", 

We have the following set of students:

--------------------------------------------------------|
Name    | Major     | KS resident   | Lives on-campus   |
--------------------------------------------------------|
Bob     | PHYS      | True          | False             |
--------------------------------------------------------|
Ava     | ECE       | True          | True              |
--------------------------------------------------------|
Jane    | CS        | False         | False             |
--------------------------------------------------------|
Steve   | MATH      | True          | True              |
--------------------------------------------------------|
Marie   | CS        | False         | True              |
--------------------------------------------------------|
Jack    | ECE       | True          | False             |
--------------------------------------------------------|

And the following predicates over our set of students:

IsCS(x) : whether student x is a CS major

InKS(x): whether student x is a Kansas resident

Campus(x): whether student x lives on campus



Consider the following predicate logic statements:

1) ∀ x (¬InCS(x))

    a) Express the statement in words

        All students are not CS majors



    b) Is the statement true or false in our set of students? Explain.

        False, Marie and Jane are CS majors, debunking condition
    


///

2) ∃ x (¬Campus(x) ⋀ InKS(x))

    a) Express the statement in words

        At least one student lives in kansas, but not on campus




    b) Is the statement true or false in our set of students? Explain.
        
        True, Bob and Jack fulfill condition



///

3) ∀ x (¬Campus(x) → InCS(x))

    a) Express the statement in words

        All students who live off campus must be CS majors



    b) Is the statement true or false in our set of students? Explain.

        False, Jack lives off campus, but is an ECE major



///

4) ¬(∃ x (Campus(x) ⋀ InCS(x)))

    a) Express the statement in words

        None of the students who live on campus and are CS majors


    b) Is the statement true or false in our set of students? Explain.

        False, Marie is a CS major who lives on campus


///

5) ¬(∀ x (InCS(x) → Campus(x)))

   a) Express the statement in words

        Not all CS students live on campus


   b) Is the statement true or false in our set of students? Explain.

        True, Jane is a CS student who lives off campus.
