Suppose you have the following propositional atoms:

p: It is sunny tomorrow
q: I will go hiking
r: I carry an umbrella
s: I am tired
t: I eat an apple


Express each of these propositional logic statements in English as
succinctly as you can.


Statement 1: p → ¬r ∧ q
If it is sunny tomorrow, I will not carry an umbrella but will go hiking.



Statement 2: ¬(r ∧ t) → ¬q
¬r v ¬t → ¬q

If I don't carry an umbrella or eat an apple, then I'm not hiking.


Statement 3: s → (¬p → ¬q)
If it isn't sunny tomorrow, then I will not go hiking if I am tired.
